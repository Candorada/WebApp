How is the value calculated?

We use current listed prices from Fortnite-API and archived prices from itemshop.gg. Offers fully included in a selected bundle are grayed out and excluded. For historical sections with missing bundle contents, every standalone item is assumed to belong to one of that section's bundles; each item links to all candidate bundles. Sections without bundles stay ungrouped. The heuristic can make mistakes, so affected dates show a yellow warning. Bundle prices still count separately unless confirmed contents prove one is covered. Missing prices keep the known V-Bucks subtotal visible and show how many listings remain unpriced. Item counts are never substituted for prices. Search changes the list only.
