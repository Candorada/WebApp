How is the value calculated?

We use current listed prices from Fortnite-API. An offer whose entire contents are included in a selected bundle is grayed out and excluded from the total. This also removes smaller bundles covered by larger ones. For identical-content bundles, the cheaper one counts. Partially overlapping bundles with unique contents still count in full. Car component listings are excluded; their purchase bundles appear under Cars. Categories match any included item type, so one bundle can appear in multiple categories but is counted at most once. Ownership discounts and cash-only packs aren't included. Search changes the list only.
